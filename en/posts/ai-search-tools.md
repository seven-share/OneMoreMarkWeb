AI search tools are changing how we find and understand information. Instead of returning only links, they summarize, cite sources, and help you continue the investigation.

## 1. Perplexity

Perplexity is useful for research-heavy searches, source-backed answers, and quick topic briefings.

### Strengths

- Answers often include citations.
- Follow-up questions feel natural.
- It works well for research and comparison tasks.

### Limitations

- The free tier has usage limits.
- You still need to judge source quality.

## 2. You.com

You.com feels more like a customizable information hub, combining search, AI answers, and utility tools.

## 3. Andi

Andi is lighter and conversational. It works well for quick understanding, but deeper research still needs verification.
